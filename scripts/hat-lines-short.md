# The Hat: short lines for voicing

Shorter version: questions up to ~6 words, reactions up to ~6 words, one thought per line.
Question reactions (r1/r2) fit any answer; a few extra lines react to one specific option (see "Reactions to one specific answer").
House names in Ukrainian follow the Ukrainian translation (Рейвенклов, Гафелпаф).
No digits or symbols in voiced text.

New ids: `unwant`, `build1`, `build2`, `s5back`, `s7forget`, `s10long`, `w3rat`. All other ids unchanged.
